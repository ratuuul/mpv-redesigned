01 Showcase

Four stills, and a skin that stays out of the way.

The skin is best seen moving, but the stills say a lot: a control bar that does not black out the picture, overlays that follow the pointer, and panels that belong to the same design.

the frames, on a light table

The four frames turn slowly on the light table. Drag the sheet to move through them by hand, or leave it alone and it keeps turning.

All four frames are captures from the same machine and the same configuration, not drawings made for this page. The control bar is the uosc Liquid Glass theme, the volume ribbon is drawn where the pointer rests, and the menu panel is set in the player's own type scale.
